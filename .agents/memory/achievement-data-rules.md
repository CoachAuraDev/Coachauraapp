---
name: CoachAura achievement data rules
description: Local milestone eligibility and the missing independent progress-record source.
---

- Calculate milestones from saved completed sessions with valid working sets and dated bodyweight entries. Exclude drafts, warm-ups, invalid dates, duplicate session IDs, and future-dated records.
- Use CoachAura's existing estimated-1RM formula and Monday-to-Sunday ISO calendar weeks; do not introduce guessed thresholds.
- The app has no separate dated progress-record model. Keep “First Progress Record” deferred unless the product adds a distinct source for it.

**Why:** The achievement gallery must reflect recorded client history rather than inventing activity or doubling another metric.

**How to apply:** Recompute visible awards from the selected client's saved history; persist only unlock/celebration metadata locally, separate from client records.
